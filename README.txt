THE LITTLE THINGS — iPhone-friendly web app

Includes:
- Home dashboard, tasks, weekly planner, bills and expense tracker
- Pet-care checklist, shopping list, creative projects, routines and notes
- Circles: create multiple circles, add member names, and preview an activity feed
- Circle sharing preference in this design: everything shared automatically with circle members
- Botanical app icon, installable PWA files, local browser storage

IMPORTANT:
Circles are currently a local prototype only. Adding a member name does not invite or connect a real person. Real shared accounts, automatic cross-device updates, authentication, secure cloud storage, and push notifications require a hosted backend and invitation flow. This starter version stores data locally in the browser on this device only.

TO INSTALL:
1. Upload the contents of this folder to an HTTPS static host.
2. Open the resulting HTTPS address in Safari on your iPhone.
3. Tap Share -> Add to Home Screen.

Do not enter sensitive financial account numbers, passwords, or other secrets.

Appointments tab: add appointments with a title, type, date, time, location/provider, and notes. Entries are saved on this device in the browser's local storage.

Groceries: separate categorized grocery checklist with quantities, saved checkmarks, copy and clear purchased items. Local storage only.

Grocery & essentials: categorized products, brand, description, custom categories and optional compressed product photos saved locally. Photos are not included in copied text lists.

Wishlist: save desired products, photos, prices, links, priorities and purchase status locally.
